01 / PROJECT TIANYUAN XINSHI

A market where feelings
change hands.

Personal project · Playable in browser

In the old city of Chang'an you buy and sell comfort, clarity, fortune and inspiration, read the market board, and open a stall of your own. The city runs on a Lua engine and is delivered to the browser through a small Node runtime, so it plays without an install.

Play in browser ↗

The free host sleeps when idle, so the first visit can take up to a minute to wake.
